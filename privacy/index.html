Privacy policy

How local saves, Apple services, sharing and support messages are handled.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Data on your device

Mochi’s Room does not require an account. Cat names, settings, discovered moments, room choices and workshop progress are stored locally on your iPhone. The app does not provide its own cloud save service. Device backups are governed by your Apple settings.

Analytics and advertising

The current app contains no advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. It does not send your gameplay history to a developer-operated server or track you across apps and websites.

Apple services

The app includes an optional cosmetic purchase feature using Apple StoreKit. Where available, Apple processes payments and provides purchase-entitlement information to the app. The developer does not receive your payment-card information through the app. Apple processes data under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

If you choose to share an illustrated keepsake, the app opens the iOS share sheet. Content is sent only to the app or destination you select, whose own privacy practices apply.

Deleting local data

Deleting the app removes its local app data from the device. Offloading the app may retain data. Any device backups or copies you share are managed separately through Apple settings or the destination you chose.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send will be processed to respond and troubleshoot your request. Email is handled by the support mailbox provider. Please do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive details. Contact the same address for questions about your support messages or to request their deletion, subject to applicable retention obligations.

This website

These static support pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, account forms or a user database. Our hosting provider, GitHub, and its infrastructure providers, may process IP addresses, browser information and request logs to deliver and secure the website. Their privacy policies apply to those hosting services.
