Common questions

How do I make my first piece?

Open Workshop from Home. Choose the first small mat and tap Start. The first mat takes about a minute. When it is ready, choose Place at home or review its sale for in-game coins.

Where is my progress saved?

On this iPhone. There is no in-app account or cross-device cloud save. Deleting the app can remove progress.

How do purchases work?

Core play is free. If an optional cosmetic pack is available, its price is shown by Apple before purchase. Use Restore purchases on the pack page to check eligible previous purchases.

What should I include in a support request?

Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, what you tapped, and what happened. Share a screenshot only if it does not contain information you want to keep private. Do not send your Apple password or payment details.
